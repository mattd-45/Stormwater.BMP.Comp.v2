        Underground utilities may block subsurface BMP placement. Relocation is often cost-prohibitive.

        High water table blocks underground infiltration systems. Dewatering adds significant cost and permitting complexity.

        Contaminated soil removal and disposal increases underground BMP costs substantially. Excavation triggers environmental handling requirements.

        Site grading limits underground BMP placement to the lowest point of the site, reducing layout flexibility.

        At-grade BMP footprint could be reprogrammed for parking, retail, or other revenue-generating use — favors rooftop or underground solutions that free up ground-level space.
